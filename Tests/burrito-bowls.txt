## 👩‍🍳 How to make it
[Photo](https://example.com/photo)
1\. Cook the rice
Rinse 250g dry rice and cook according to packet instructions. Fluff and set aside. This will be your base layer.
2\. Make the burrito beef
Dice the onion and pepper. Fry with 1 tbsp oil for 4–5 minutes. Add the mince, breaking it up until browned and cooked through.
3\. Make it saucy
Add the paprika, cumin, chilli powder, garlic granules and tomato purée. Stir for 1 minute. Add the entire tin of tomatoes, drained black beans and drained sweetcorn. Simmer for 10–12 minutes until thick, seasoning to taste.
4\. Assemble four bowls
Divide the rice between 4 containers. Spoon over the beef and bean mixture, then sprinkle each with roughly 37g grated cheddar. Keep the layers distinct.
