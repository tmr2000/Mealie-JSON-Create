Desk lunch couscous
scribbled this down on my phone
WHAT I HAVE
couscous
120 g
chickpeas
1 x 400g tin, drained
boiling water
150 ml
1 tbsp olive oil
half a lemon, juiced
DO THIS
Put the couscous in a bowl.
Pour over 150 ml boiling water and cover for 5 minutes.
Fluff with a fork.
Mix in the chickpeas, olive oil and lemon juice.
Divide into 2 lunch boxes.
NOTES TO SELF
No freezing instructions supplied.
