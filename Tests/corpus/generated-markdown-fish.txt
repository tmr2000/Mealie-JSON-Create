## Lemon fish with beans
Serves 2
**Ingredients**
### Fish
- 2 cod fillets (about 300g total)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 lemon, sliced
### Beans
- 1 x 400g tin cannellini beans, drained
- 2 tbsp water
**Method**
1. Preheat the oven to 200C. Put the cod, oil and lemon in a baking dish.
2. Bake for 12-15 minutes until the fish is cooked through.
3. Warm the beans with 2 tbsp water in a saucepan. Serve beside the fish.
Notes
Do not freeze the assembled dish.
