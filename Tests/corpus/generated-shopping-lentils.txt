# Smoky lentil pots
3 portions, freezer friendly
## Shopping list
5 of 5 ingredients checked
Reset
Red lentils
200g dry
Chopped tomatoes
1 x 400g tin
Vegetable stock
600ml
Onion
1 whole
Smoked paprika
2 tsp
Estimated UK shopping cost: £3
## How to make it
[Photo](https://example.test/lentils.jpg)
1. Cook the onion.
Dice the onion and soften it in a splash of the stock for 5 minutes.
2. Simmer the lentils.
Stir in lentils, tomatoes, remaining stock and paprika. Simmer for 20-25 minutes.
3. Portion up.
Divide between 3 containers.
