Simple Sesame Rice
Prep time: 5 minutes
Cook time: 20 minutes
Total time: 25 minutes
Servings: 4 (3/4 cup each)
Ingredients
1 clove garlic
1 Tbsp butter
1 cup long grain jasmine rice
1.75 cups water
1 Tbsp soy sauce
1/2 Tbsp toasted sesame oil
1 tsp sesame seeds
Method
Chop the garlic. Soften it in butter over medium heat for 1-2 minutes.

Stir in the rice and toast for another 2-3 minutes.

Add water and soy sauce. Stir, cover and bring to a boil; reduce the heat and simmer for 15 minutes.

Switch off the heat and leave covered for 5 minutes. Fluff, mix in sesame oil and seeds, and serve warm.
