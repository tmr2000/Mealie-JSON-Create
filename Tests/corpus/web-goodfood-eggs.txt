Perfect scrambled eggs
Total time: 10 minutes
Ingredients
2 large free range eggs
6 tbsp single cream or full cream milk
a knob of butter
Method
Beat the eggs with the cream or milk and a pinch of salt until combined.

Warm a non-stick pan for about a minute. Melt the butter without browning it.

Add the eggs. Wait 20 seconds, then lift and fold them using a wooden spoon.

Pause for 10 seconds, then fold again.

Continue until softly set with some runny patches. Take off the heat and briefly allow residual cooking.

Stir once more and serve immediately.
