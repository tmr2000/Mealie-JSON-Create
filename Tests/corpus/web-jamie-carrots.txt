The best whole-baked carrots
Serves: 4
Ingredients
750g/1lb 10oz young bunched carrots, washed and scrubbed
olive oil
herb or red wine vinegar
sea salt and freshly ground black pepper
a few sprigs of fresh thyme
3 cloves of garlic, crushed
Method
Heat the oven to 200°C/400°F/gas 6. Coat carrots in oil, a little vinegar, seasoning, thyme and crushed garlic. Cover a roasting dish tightly with foil and bake for 30 to 40 minutes until tender. Uncover and roast for 10 minutes more to brown.
