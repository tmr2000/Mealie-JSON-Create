### Easy no-waste slow cooker chicken curry — 4 portions

**Ingredients**

- 500g boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into chunks
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 × 400g tin chopped tomatoes
- 1 × 400g tin coconut milk
- 3 tbsp curry powder — mild or medium
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 chicken stock cube
- 1 × 400g tin chickpeas, drained
- 200g fresh spinach
- Salt and pepper
- 300g basmati rice, to serve

**Method**

1. Put the **onion, garlic, tomatoes, coconut milk, curry powder, cumin and crumbled stock cube** into the slow cooker and stir.
2. Add the **chicken and chickpeas** and stir again.
3. Cook on **LOW for 6–7 hours** or **HIGH for 3–4 hours**, until the chicken is cooked through and tender.
4. Stir in the **whole bag of spinach** for the final 10–15 minutes. Taste and season.
5. Cook **300g rice** according to the packet and divide everything between four portions.
